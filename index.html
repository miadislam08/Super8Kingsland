About the hotel

A simple, comfortable stop on the Georgia coast

Whether you're driving the I-95 corridor, visiting family at Kings Bay, or heading out to explore Georgia's coast, Super 8 by Wyndham Kingsland gives you a clean, easy place to land.

Start your morning with free coffee and a breakfast item, stay connected with free WiFi, and park for free, including space for RVs and trailers. Every room has a microwave and refrigerator, and kitchenettes, guest laundry, and early check-in or late check-out are available, and pets are welcome. Every stay booked through Wyndham can earn Wyndham Rewards points.

Location

Staying in Kingsland, Georgia

Kingsland is the first stop in Georgia for many northbound I-95 drivers, and the hotel is just off Exit 3, so you're off the interstate and checked in fast. It's a convenient base for visits to Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay, the historic waterfront in nearby St. Marys, the ferry to Cumberland Island National Seashore, and day trips to Jacksonville.
